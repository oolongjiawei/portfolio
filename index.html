Toronto Building Permits, 1991–2025

For George Brown's data-storytelling course, our team analyzed 35 years of the City of Toronto's open building-permit data and built an operational dashboard and an executive scorecard in Tableau for a permit-services client.

  • 19,995 permits issued in 2024 (−3,612 YoY), while average time to issuance improved to ~51 days (−15.5%).
  • A classic 80/20: about 20% of permit types account for ~80% of total permit duration.
  • New Building permits are the slowest, often more than a year from application to issuance.
  • Activity clusters in a few neighbourhoods: Willowdale, East York, Greektown, the Annex and Dufferin Grove.
  • Tableau
  • TabPy (Python)
  • KPIs & YoY
  • What-if parameters
  • Pareto
  • Clustering
  • Regression

02 — About

From rigs
to interfaces

“A rig is a user interface for animators.”

If the controls are confusing, even the most elegant system fails. So I design for the person using it, sweat the small details, and listen before I build.

As a rigger I learned fast by asking questions early and often. At Kinectrics I worked from verbal briefs, demoed at every meeting and iterated on feedback. And Karkumi Café, whose first website I designed and built, brought me back to improve their current one.

I retrained through BrainStation's software engineering bootcamp and George Brown's Applied AI program (Honours), and I'm looking for front-end developer roles where the details of how something looks and feels matter, and I'm curious about data analysis too.
