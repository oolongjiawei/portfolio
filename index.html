02 — About

From rigs to models

“Technical complexity means nothing if the user experience fails.”

A rig is a user interface for animators. If the controls are confusing, the most elegant joint hierarchy in the world still slows the whole show down. Five years of that taught me to treat every logical structure as an experience someone has to live with.

I retrained through BrainStation's software engineering bootcamp and George Brown's Applied AI program (Honours), then joined Kinectrics as an AI developer co-op, improving an internal RAG tool — its retrieval workflows, evidence traceability, and the interface people used every day.

I'm looking for roles in AI / ML application development, front-end, or full-stack — teams where the model and the user both matter.
